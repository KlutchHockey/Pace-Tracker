Export All Garmin Activities

Garmin allows full bulk account exports directly from their data center.

  1. Sign in to your account at garmin.com/account/datamanagement.
  2. Under Data Management, select Export Your Data, then click Request Data Export.
  3. Garmin will prepare your files and email you a secure download link.
  4. Unzip the downloaded folder and locate your running activity files.
  5. Click Upload Activities Batch in this app and select all files at once to calculate your metrics.
Tip: TCX and GPX files can be highlighted and batch-loaded all at once.
